Split buttons

Compose's split button, ported from SplitButton.kt: a main action and, 2dp away, a button that opens more. The halves share rounded outer ends; their inner corners grow when pressed. Checked, the trailing button turns fully round, darkens slightly and turns its chevron over (the menu itself comes with menus at step 58).

The four split buttons

Sizes

Extra small to extra large (32 to 136dp), with SplitButtonDefaults' paddings, icon sizes and inner corners for each, and ButtonDefaults' text style.
