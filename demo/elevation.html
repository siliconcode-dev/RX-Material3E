Elevation

The six Material elevation levels. Each is two shadows from the M3 spec, a sharp key shadow and a soft ambient one. <rx-elevation> takes a level, or any distance through --rx-elevation, and animates between them.

In the dark theme the shadows are faint on purpose. Material shows depth there mostly through lighter surface colors (surface-container and up), as on Android.
