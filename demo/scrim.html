Scrim

Compose's Scrim: the scrim color at 32% over the content behind a modal surface. It fades in and out on the default effects spring. When it can dismiss the modal, it is a labelled button for screen readers (as in Compose, it is not a Tab stop; the modal closes on Escape too).

The sheet here moves as Compose's ModalBottomSheet does: up on the default spatial spring, down on the fast effects spring, while the scrim fades on the default effects spring.
