Buttons

Compose's five buttons, ported from Button.kt and its tokens: the colors, elevations, padding and sizes of ButtonDefaults. The container is 40dp tall in a 48dp touch target, as Compose's clickable Surface lays it out. Hover rises and falls with Compose's elevation tweens; press shows the ripple; Tab shows the focus ring.

Sizes

The five expressive sizes, laid out as Compose's size samples: the container height (32, 40, 56, 96 and 136dp) with ButtonDefaults' padding, icon size, icon spacing and text style for it. shape="square" is ButtonDefaults.squareShape, 12dp corners at every size.

Press to morph

With morph the corners spring to the pressed shape of ButtonDefaults.shapesFor while the button is held (8dp up to small, 12dp medium, 16dp large and extra large), on the default effects spring, so there is no bounce. Let go half way and it turns back from where it is. Keyboard presses morph too.

Emphasis in use

One filled button for the main action, tonal or outlined for the next ones, text buttons for the least important, as in dialogs.
