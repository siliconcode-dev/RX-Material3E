Buttons

Compose's five buttons, ported from Button.kt and its tokens: the colors, elevations, padding and sizes of ButtonDefaults. The container is 40dp tall in a 48dp touch target, as Compose's clickable Surface lays it out. Hover rises and falls with Compose's elevation tweens; press shows the ripple; Tab shows the focus ring.
