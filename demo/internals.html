Building blocks

The shared pieces every component is built from. They are exported too, for building your own components that behave like the library's.

Forms and keyboard

The checkboxes and buttons below are custom elements made only from these pieces: semantics on the element itself, native keyboard behaviour (Space toggles a checkbox; Enter and Space press a button), and form association through ElementInternals. They submit, reset, follow <fieldset disabled>, are labelled by <label> and take part in validation.

One Tab stop for a group

A roving tabindex: Tab enters the group once, the arrow keys move inside it (swapped in right-to-left), Home and End jump to the ends, and disabled items are skipped. Try it with the RTL switch at the top.

Touch targets

Compose's minimumInteractiveComponentSize: a control is laid out at least 48dp each way with its visible part centered, so small controls are still easy to hit. The dashed line is the touch target. --rx-minimum-interactive-component-size: 0 turns it off.
