Icon buttons

Compose's four icon buttons, ported from IconButton.kt and IconButtonDefaults.kt: standard, filled, tonal and outlined, each also as a toggle. The container is 40dp in a 48dp touch target. Standard and outlined icon buttons take the color of what they sit on; their vibrant colors are fixed. With morph the corners pinch in while held and, on a toggle, go squarer when on.

Toggles

A toggle is a checkbox for assistive technology and forms: Space toggles it. Click to switch them; the last column morphs, and its standard and outlined toggles use the vibrant colors, as in Compose.

On colored surfaces

Standard and outlined icon buttons follow the content color around them (Compose's LocalContentColor), so they fit any bar.
