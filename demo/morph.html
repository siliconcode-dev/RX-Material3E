Shape morphing

Google's Morph from androidx.graphics.shapes, ported by hand. It pairs the corners of two shapes, cuts curves so both outlines line up, then blends them. Every number matches Android bit for bit: the port does Kotlin's 32-bit Float math step by step, checked against Google's own Kotlin for all 1,225 pairs of Material shapes.

Tap a shape

<rx-shape> morphs whenever its shape changes, with the expressive default spatial spring. Change your mind halfway and it runs back.
