Toggle buttons

Compose's four toggle buttons, ported from ToggleButton.kt: a button that stays on or off. Off, it is round; on, it takes its checked colors and square corners; held down, its corners pinch in. The corners spring between the three shapes on the fast spatial spring, so they bounce a little. The outlined one loses its outline when on, the outline shrinking and fading.

The four toggle buttons

Off On, with icon Disabled

Sizes

Extra small to extra large, small by default. Each size has its own height, padding, icon size, text style, and pressed and checked corners (from 6dp pressed and 12dp checked at small to 16dp and 28dp at extra large).

In a form

For assistive technology and forms it is a checkbox: Space toggles it, and when it is on it submits its value under its name. A form reset puts it back as the page started it.
