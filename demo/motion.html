Motion

Compose's springs, ported by hand: SpringSimulation, its duration estimate and Animatable. Values and durations are checked against Google's Kotlin, number for number. The spring tokens in CSS come from the same code.

The six springs

Spatial springs move 100dp, effects springs change an alpha from 0 to 1. A spring ends when it stays within 0.01 of its target, as in Compose, so a longer move takes longer.

Animatable

Tap the track. The ball springs there with Animatable; tap again while it moves and it carries its speed into the new target, as Compose does.

Web Animations

animateWithSpring() runs ordinary element.animate() keyframes on a spring's curve, with the duration Compose would use for that distance.
