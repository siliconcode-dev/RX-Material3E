Button groups

Compose's ButtonGroup, ported from ButtonGroup.kt: buttons, toggle buttons and icon buttons in a row, 12dp apart. Press and hold one: it grows into its neighbours on the fast spatial spring and settles back when you let go. Each button keeps its own width; icon buttons fill their 48dp touch target, as in Compose.
