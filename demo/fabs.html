FABs

Compose's floating action buttons, ported from FloatingActionButton.kt: the screen's main action, an icon on a raised container. It sits at elevation level 3 and rises to level 4 under the pointer.

Sizes

The small FAB is the older 40dp one; medium is new in M3 Expressive. Each size has its own shape and icon size.

Colors

M3 Expressive's six colors, primary container first (the default), and baseline M3's surface FAB.

Lowered

Compose's loweredElevation: level 1, level 2 under the pointer.
