FAB menus

Compose's FAB menu, ported from FloatingActionButtonMenu.kt: a toggle FAB that opens up to six related actions above it. The FAB morphs into a round close button while the items appear one by one from the bottom.

The menu

Compose's sample: Reply, Reply all, Forward, Snooze, Archive and Label. Choosing an item or pressing Escape closes the menu. With the keyboard, Tab or the down arrow goes from the button to the top item.

    add close

    Toggle FAB sizes

    The toggle FAB starts as a baseline (56dp), medium (80dp) or large (96dp) FAB and always ends as the 56dp round close button with a 20dp icon. The smaller button keeps to the top-end corner of the starting size. Click them.

    Color sets

    Primary (the default), secondary and tertiary: the button goes from the container color to the full color, and the items use the container color.

    Alignment

    The items and the button line up at the end (the default), the start or the center.
